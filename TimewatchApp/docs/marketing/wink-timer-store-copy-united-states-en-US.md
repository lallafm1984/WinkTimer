## 전체 설명

```text
Wink Timer is an everyday timekeeping app for alarms, timers, and stopwatch timing.

When your hands are busy, time should still be easy to pause, resume, and record. If your hands are wet while cooking or you cannot tap the screen during a workout, use look, wink, and flip modes to control timers and stopwatch actions.

Set alarms for wake-ups, classes, meetings, workouts, breaks, and routines that need a reminder. Alarms support daily repeats, weekday schedules, date alarms, sound, volume, vibration, and snooze.

Use the timer when you need to count down a set amount of time. Use the stopwatch when you need elapsed time and lap records.

Key features

- Timer: start, pause, resume, and reset with buttons
- Alarms: daily repeats, weekday schedules, date alarms, sound, volume, vibration, and snooze
- Alarm management: create, enable, disable, edit, and delete multiple alarms
- Alarm ringing screen: stop an alarm or choose snooze
- Stopwatch: measure elapsed time and record laps
- Timer alerts: sound and vibration for countdown completion
- No-touch controls: use look, wink, and device flip modes for supported timekeeping actions
- LOOK PAUSE: pause and resume based on whether you are looking at the screen
- WINK CONTROL: use wink gestures for timer actions and lap records
- FLIP TIMER: flip your device face down to start, then face up to pause
- SMILE MODE: use smile detection for timer control
- History: review recent timing records and timeline events

Wink Timer is not limited to focus or study sessions. Use it as an alarm clock, cooking timer, workout timer, study timer, work timer, break timer, or quick stopwatch for everyday routines.

Camera-based features may vary by device performance, lighting, face angle, and camera position.
```
