## 전체 설명

```text
Use a timer more comfortably without touching the screen.

Wink Timer is a timekeeping app with a countdown timer, stopwatch, and alarms in one place. Set the time like a normal timer, then use wink, look, smile, or phone flip controls when you are working out, cooking, studying, or working and do not want to tap the screen.

Use the no-touch timer when your hands are wet, you are wearing gloves, you need to hold your workout position, or you do not want to break focus.

Key features

Countdown timer

- Set any countdown time you need
- Start, pause, resume, and reset
- Sound and vibration when the timer ends
- Useful for workouts, cooking, study, breaks, and routines

No-touch controls

- Wink mode: control timer actions with a wink
- Look mode: pause while looking at the screen, resume when you look away
- Smile mode: control the timer with smile detection
- Flip mode: control timer actions by turning your phone over

Stopwatch

- Measure elapsed time
- Record lap times
- Useful for workouts, study, tests, and repeated tasks

Alarms

- Set wake-up, class, meeting, workout, and break alarms
- Daily repeats, weekday repeats, and date alarms
- Alarm sound, volume, vibration, and snooze settings
- Create, edit, enable, disable, and delete multiple alarms

History

- Review recent timer and stopwatch records
- Check the timekeeping flows you use often

Use Wink Timer when:

- It is hard to tap the screen during a workout
- Your hands are wet while cooking
- You want to manage time without breaking study or work focus
- You are timing stretching, meditation, rest, or routines
- You want a stopwatch and alarms together
- You need to measure repeated tasks quickly

Wink Timer adds wink, look, smile, and flip controls to a basic countdown timer, stopwatch, and alarm app.

Camera-based no-touch features may vary by device performance, lighting, face angle, and camera position.
```
